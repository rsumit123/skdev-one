Counter cycle: Tank beats Gunner · Gunner beats AT Team · AT Team beats Tank.
Gunner does double damage to light infantry. AT Team does quadruple damage to armour. Tanks hit everything in range at once, so they shred swarms.

Infantry is the cheap generalist — beats AT Teams, loses to Gunners and Tanks.
Chopper flies: only Gunners (weakly) and AT Teams (hard) can hit it, so it shreds tanks and gunners — but it barely dents a base, so you still need ground units to break through.

Economy costs 45 (price rises each time) and adds +0.5 to base income, up to three times. Income also escalates on its own — it roughly doubles by the one-minute mark and keeps climbing, so no stalemate holds.
